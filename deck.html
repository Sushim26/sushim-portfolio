How I work

Most hiring problems are not sourcing problems. They are decision problems — unclear roles, inconsistent assessment, and no record of why anyone was hired.

I build hiring and people systems the way a product team would: a defined pipeline, written scorecards, instrumentation at each stage, and a record that survives the people who created it. That applies whether the mandate is one founding engineer or a full review cycle across 200+ employees.

Seven years span agency and in-house. Four of those were global RPO at Techaxis, running executive search for Fortune 100/500 enterprises in the US. The rest has been in-house at hoichoi, LoglineAI and Sooper, owning everything from the first conversation to the exit checklist.

Executive & Founding Team Hiring

End-to-end mandates for the roles that set a company's direction — founding engineers, CTOs, CFOs, SVPs and senior directors.

Starts with role architecture, not a job post: what the role actually has to deliver in its first year, what it is accountable for, and what good looks like at offer stage. From there, AI-assisted sourcing, structured assessment, and a scorecard every interviewer files against.

Comp is benchmarked against live market data before the first conversation, so offers land at the right number rather than being negotiated upward. Onboarding is treated as part of the mandate — a hire is not delivered until they are productive.

Performance & Governance Systems

A performance model that weights OKRs, Efficiency, AI Usage and the Keeper Test equally — deployed as working review software, not a template.

The 25/25/25/25 split forces a complete picture: what someone delivered, how efficiently, how well they have adopted the tools available, and whether you would fight to keep them. Equal weighting stops any single dimension from carrying a review on its own.

Shipped as software rather than a document, so the cycle actually completes. Managers file in a consistent format, calibration happens against comparable records, and the output is a record you can reference a year later.

Model
25% OKRs · 25% Efficiency · 25% AI Usage · 25% Keeper Test
Includes

People Operations & Lifecycle Design

HR operations, compliance, payroll and the full employee lifecycle — designed so the handoffs between them do not leak.

Onboarding is 28 tracked tasks with stage gates, a named buddy, manager visibility and a 90-day plan agreed before Day 1. Progress per joiner is visible on a dashboard, so a stalled induction is caught in week one rather than at the first review.

Offboarding gets the same treatment: a real checklist covering access, assets, handover and an exit conversation that produces something usable, instead of the scramble that usually follows a resignation.

People Analytics & Talent Intelligence

Pipeline and workforce data presented so leadership can act on it, rather than a monthly deck nobody opens.

Live dashboards cover time-to-fill, offer acceptance, stage-by-stage drop-off and attrition trends, benchmarked against the market you are hiring in. When a stage starts leaking, it shows up as a number before it shows up as a missed quarter.

How the pipeline runs

Five stages, each instrumented. Filter early so senior time is spent late.

  1. 01

    Sourcing & talent mapping

    Boolean search across LinkedIn Recruiter, Juicebox, Wellfound and Eightfold.ai, surfacing passive candidates as well as applicants.

    Full market
  2. 02

    Async assessment, AI-scored

    Video, aptitude and technical assessments completed asynchronously. Scoring is consistent and removes scheduling overhead before anyone's time is committed.

    ~60% filtered
  3. 03

    Structured interview & debrief

    Competency rubrics per role family, trained interviewers, and recorded debriefs that become scorecards rather than opinions.

    Scorecard filed
  4. 04

    Offer & negotiation

    Comp benchmarked in advance. Offers generated through a structured workflow with full CTC breakdown, tracked in the ATS.

    85%+ accepted
  5. 05

    Onboarding & 90-day plan

    28 tracked tasks with stage gates, a named buddy, manager visibility and a live view of progress per joiner.

    20 day average
