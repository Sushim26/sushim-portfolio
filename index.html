  2. 02~60% drop-off

    Async assessment, AI-scored

    Candidates complete async video, aptitude and technical assessments. AI scoring removes scheduling overhead and standardises evaluation before human time is spent.

    Equip.co · micro1 · Mettl · HackerRank · UnStop
  3. 03

    Structured interview + debrief

    Competency rubrics mapped per role family, interviewer training, and recorded debriefs that convert into structured scorecards rather than opinions.

    tldv · custom scorecard · ATS
  4. 0485%+ accepted

    Offer & negotiation

    Comp benchmarked against market data. Offer letters generated via a structured workflow with CTC breakdowns, tracked in the ATS.

    ATS · comp benchmarking · offer workflow
  5. 05

    Onboarding & 90-day plan

    A 28-task induction with stage gates, buddy structure, manager visibility and a live dashboard showing progress per joiner.
